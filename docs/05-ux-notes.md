# UX Notes

Running list of UX/UI ideas and principles. Ideas land here first, then become requirements when they're ready.

## Core principle
Checking in should feel quick and a little like a game, never a chore. Say what comes to mind and move on. The app leads the user (a directed experience), so they don't get stuck overthinking what to enter. Anxiety about "what do I say" is what makes people snooze or abandon trackers.

## Ideas
| Idea | Status | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Optional timer per question | Idea (FR-33, Could) | Offered as a tip the user can turn on. Nudges quick answers. |
| Mood-only check-in counts | Decided (FR-10) | One tap is enough. Lowers the bar on low-energy days. |
| Short note, 280 characters | Decided (FR-15) | Keeps it light. |
| Offer export before account deletion | Decided (FR-24) | Respectful exit. Part of the data governance story. |
